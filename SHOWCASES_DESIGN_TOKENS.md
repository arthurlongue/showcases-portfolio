# Master Reference: Showcase Niches & Design Tokens

This document serves as the master reference list for every showcase niche built in this project, linking each to its foundational design template and the core visual tokens that define its aesthetic.

The `design/` folder is organized into two main directories:
- `design/used/`: The active design templates that have been implemented in the routes below.
- `design/unused/`: The archived design templates saved for future showcases.

**Implementation Note:**
